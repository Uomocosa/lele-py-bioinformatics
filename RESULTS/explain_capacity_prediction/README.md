# Simple Guide to Capacity Predictions (SHAP Outputs)

_AI Explenation_

When our script predicts the capacity of a polymer, it also uses a tool called **SHAP** to explain *why* it gave that prediction. 

Imagine the model starts every polymer at a baseline score, and then adds or subtracts points based on the polymer's specific features (like its chemical structure or molecular weight). The files generated by this script simply show you how those points were awarded.

Here is the simple breakdown of what each file means and how to read it.

---

### 1. `shap_summary_bar.png` (The Overall Leaderboard)
**What it is:** A simple bar chart showing which features matter the most overall across all the polymers you just tested. 

**How to read it (Top to Bottom):** The feature at the very top is the most important deciding factor for the model. 
* **Bar Length:** The longer the bar, the more heavily the model relies on that feature to make its guess. 
* *Note: This chart just tells you WHAT is important, but not if it increases or decreases the capacity.*

---

### 2. `shap_summary.png` (The Impact Plot)
**What it is:** Often called a "beeswarm plot," this graph shows exactly *how* a feature changes the score. Does it make the capacity go up or down?

**How to read it:**
* **The Center Line (0):** This is the neutral zone. 
* **Left vs. Right:** Dots pushed to the **right** mean that feature *increased* the predicted capacity. Dots pushed to the **left** mean it *decreased* the capacity.
* **Red vs. Blue:** **Red** means the polymer has a *high* amount of that feature (e.g., lots of carbon rings). **Blue** means it has a *low* amount.
* **Example:** If you look at a feature and see a cluster of **Red dots on the Right side**, it means: *"Having a LOT of this feature creates a HIGHER capacity."*

---

### 3. `polymer_comparison.csv` (The Quick Summary Cheat Sheet)
**What it is:** A high-level overview table of your results. It's the best place to start if you just want the bottom line.

**How to read it:**
* **`polymer_smiles`**: The chemical structure of the polymer tested.
* **`predicted_capacity`**: The final score (capacity) the model guessed.
* **`top_5_features`**: The 5 specific features that had the biggest hand in deciding the score for *that specific polymer*. 

---

### 4. `top_features.csv` (The Detailed Scorecard)
**What it is:** The raw math behind the summary table. It lists the exact "points" added or subtracted by every single feature for every single polymer.

**How to read it:**
* Find your polymer using the **`polymer_idx`** (which matches the comparison table).
* Look at the **`shap_value`**. This is the exact number of points that feature contributed. 
* **Example:** If the `shap_value` is `+4.5`, it means that specific feature bumped the polymer's capacity up by 4.5 units. If it is `-2.1`, the feature dragged the capacity down by 2.1 units.
